Rental requirements

What you need to bring

  • Minimum age 21 (23 for the Grand Starex)
  • Valid driving licence (P licence holders: please ask us first)
  • MyKad for Malaysians, or passport for foreign guests
  • International Driving Permit if your licence is not in English or Malay
  • Small refundable deposit, returned after the car comes back

FAQ

Good questions

Can you deliver the car to my house?

Yes. We deliver to homes, condos and offices across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor. Choose "Doorstep delivery" and share your address on WhatsApp.

Can I drive to my kampung or another state?

Yes. Unlimited mileage within Peninsular Malaysia. Just let us know your destination for long trips.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, baby seats and boosters are available. Mention the age of your child when you enquire.

Can I rent for a week or a month?

Absolutely. Weekly and monthly rentals are popular with families and people waiting for a new car. Ask us on WhatsApp.

Can you meet me at KLIA?

Yes. Select KLIA or KLIA2 and send your flight details. We will meet you at arrivals.

What do I do if I get a flat tyre or breakdown?

Message or call us on WhatsApp any time. Roadside assistance is included, and we will help you get moving again.
